About MAGICS

Shaping Tomorrow's Visionary Leaders

Operating under the prestigious Ma'din Academy, Ma'din MAGICS is dedicated to identifying promising young minds right after SSLC/10th Grade and grooming them for public governance, civil administration, and high-impact international careers.

Values-Driven Leadership

We focus on developing morally grounded, socially responsible individuals who combine high intellectual competence with solid integrity to create positive societal change.

Spiritual Mentorship

Guided by the spiritual vision and patronage of Sayyid Ibraheemul Khaleel Al Bukhari (Khaleel Thangal), students receive continuous spiritual discipline and moral guidance.

Serene Campus Environment

Situated in a peaceful, eco-friendly green campus in Malappuram, providing a serene, distraction-free environment for concentrated study and personal development.

What We Provide

Comprehensive 4-Pillar Curriculum

A well-structured residential system covering academics, competitive exams, moral sciences, languages, and physical training.

Pillar 01

Integrated Academics & IAS Coaching

  • Higher Secondary Stream (+1 & +2): Science, Commerce, and Humanities streams aligned with board standards.
  • Civil Services Foundation: Early intensive coaching for UPSC CSE, Kerala PSC, SSC, and CUET-UG university entrance tests.
Pillar 02

Comprehensive Islamic Studies

  • Ethical & Heritage Training: Deep study of Qur'an, Hadith, Islamic Fiqh, History, and Moral Science.
  • Harmonious Living: Training students to bridge high administrative aspirations with ethical integrity and spiritual devotion.
Guided by spiritual discipline and continuous ethical orientation.
Pillar 03

Foreign Languages & Communication

  • Multilingual Training: Professional instruction in German, Spanish, and advanced English to build international exposure.
  • Public Speaking & Debate: Creative writing, literary seminars, mock parliamentary debates, and confidence-building drills.
German & Spanish certified curriculum tracks.
Pillar 04

Physical Fitness & Mental Wellness

  • Sports Academy: Dedicated football coaching ⚽ and athletic routines for physical endurance.
  • Mental Wellbeing: Structured recreational routines, outdoor activities, and stress-management mentorship.
Daily fitness drills, swimming & football sessions.
